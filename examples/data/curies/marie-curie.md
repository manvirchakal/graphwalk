# Marie Curie

Marie Curie was a physicist and chemist who conducted pioneering research on
radioactivity. She was born in Warsaw in 1867 and moved to Paris in 1891 to study at
the University of Paris.

Marie Curie married Pierre Curie in 1895. She was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize,
sharing the 1903 Nobel Prize in Physics with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel. In 1911
she won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the discovery of polonium and radium.
